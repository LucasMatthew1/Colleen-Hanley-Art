# Colleen Hanley Art - Design Direction

## Concept
**Coastal Play / Handcrafted Ocean**
The site should feel like a page from a children's book brought to life. It's not a cold gallery; it's a warm, sunny day at the beach.

## Metaphor
A sun-drenched coastal porch where art and stories are shared. Hand-drawn sketches meet professional oil paintings.

## Palette
- **Primary BG:** Slate Blue (#334155) - Deep and calming like the ocean at dusk.
- **Accent 1:** Sunny Yellow (#FBBF24) - For energy and warmth.
- **Accent 2:** Sky Blue (#BAE6FD) - For lightness and breathability.
- **Accent 3:** Brown (#634832) - Earthy, for the owl (Boaz).
- **Accent 4:** Orange (#FB923C) - Playful details.

## Typography
- **Display:** "Varela Round" - Soft, rounded, friendly, and approachable.
- **Body:** "Nunito Sans" - Clean, legible, and slightly rounded to match the display face.

## Motion
- **Floating Boaz:** A gentle floating animation for the owl mascot.
- **Ocean Waves:** SVG wave dividers between sections.
- **Soft Reveals:** Elements fade and slide up gently (0.9s duration, expo-out).

## Layout
Editorial but playful. Mix of professional photography, book illustrations, and masonry galleries.
